Checking a figure yourself

  1. Open the row and follow its endpoint: the same address the refresh called, at the source, not a copy we host.
  2. Compare the year you care about with the raw values in the row. Monthly and quarterly sources become one value a year (an average, the last month of a stock, or a total) and the row says which.
  3. Read the source release for definitions, revisions and footnotes. The notes in the row point at what to look for.
  4. Or rerun the whole thing. Clone the repository and run python ingest/ingest.py --dry-run: it fetches everything fresh and reports what it got.

Sources revise their own history, which is normal for official statistics, and the next refresh picks that up. Anything else is a bug: tell us.
