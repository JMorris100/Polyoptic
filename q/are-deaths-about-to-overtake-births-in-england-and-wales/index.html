Are deaths about to overtake births in England and Wales?

Not yet, but nearly: births outnumbered deaths in England and Wales by just 14,000 in 2025, against 363,000 at the post-war peak.

Source: ONS Births in England and Wales; ONS Deaths registered in England and Wales · Open Government Licence v3.0

The numbers

In 1947 births outnumbered deaths in England and Wales by 363,000. In 2025 the gap was 14,000: 585,000 births against 571,000 deaths. Deaths last outnumbered births in 1977, one of only two years since 1938 when they did.

Why a gap chart

The question is about the difference between two lines. A gap chart shades it, so you read births minus deaths — the natural change in the population — directly, and see how close the lines have come.

Read it carefully

England and Wales only. With the gap this narrow, population growth now comes almost entirely from migration.
