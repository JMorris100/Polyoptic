Did UK productivity stop growing after 2008?

Nearly: output per hour grew 2.2% a year from 1971 to 2007, and 0.4% a year since.

Source: ONS Labour Productivity · Open Government Licence v3.0

Open this in the explorer → See it with the other questions

The numbers

Output per hour grew by 2.2% a year on average from 1971 to 2007, and 0.4% a year from 2007 to 2025. Had the old rate carried on, the index would be about 138 today rather than 99 — roughly 39% higher.

Why a line chart

One series over half a century. A line shows the change of slope — the bend at 2008 — and the bend is the whole story.

The indicators

  • Labour productivity99.3 index, 2023=100, 2025 · UK

Read it carefully

Productivity is output per hour worked across the whole economy. It is hard to measure and is revised often, so the last year or two may move.
