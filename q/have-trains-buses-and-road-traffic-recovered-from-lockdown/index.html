Have UK trains, buses and road traffic recovered from lockdown?

Rail and road traffic have, buses haven’t: against 2010 = 100, rail 135, road traffic 113 and buses 79.

Source: ORR passenger rail usage; DfT bus statistics; DfT road safety statistics · Open Government Licence v3.0

Open this in the explorer → See it with the other questions

The numbers

Set each to 2010 = 100. Before the pandemic, in 2018-19, rail journeys stood at 130 and road traffic at 110, while bus journeys had fallen to 93. Lockdown took rail down to 29 and buses to 33. By 2025-26 rail was back to 135 and traffic 113, but buses only 79.

Why a line chart

Journeys, journeys and vehicle miles: three units at three very different sizes. Rebasing each to 100 in the same year puts them on one axis as growth, so ‘back to normal’ means the same thing for all three.

Read it carefully

Rail and bus figures are April-to-March years and road traffic is calendar years, so the pandemic dip lands a few months apart. Rail has several changes of method, marked in the explorer.
