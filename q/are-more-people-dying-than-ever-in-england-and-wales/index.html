Are more people dying than ever in England and Wales?

No: 571,000 people died in England and Wales in 2025, fewer than in 1918, and within 1% of 1971 although the UK population has grown 24%.

Source: ONS Deaths registered in England and Wales · Open Government Licence v3.0

The numbers

Deaths in England and Wales have stayed between 445,000 (1923) and 612,000 (1918) every year since 1900. In 2025 there were 571,000. Since 1971 the UK population has grown by 24%, yet deaths in 2025 were within 1% of the 567,000 in 1971.

Why a line chart

The axis starts at zero, which shows a flat line for what it is. Why it is flat is the surprise: far more people, each living far longer.
