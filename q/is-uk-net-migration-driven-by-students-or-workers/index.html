Is UK net migration driven by students or workers?

Both: study visas peaked at 624,000 in 2022 and work visas at 614,000 in 2023, and both have fallen since, with net migration down to 171,000 in 2025.

Source: ONS Long-term international migration; Home Office entry clearance visa statistics · Open Government Licence v3.0 · a year still in progress is left out

The numbers

Net migration was 184,000 in 2019, peaked at 891,000 in 2022 and was 171,000 in 2025. Study visas peaked first, at 624,000 in 2022; work visas at 614,000 in 2023, when 348,000 health and care visas were granted. Both routes have fallen since, in step with the headline number.

Why a line chart

All four count people, so one axis is fair — and putting the routes beside the headline number shows which of them moved with it, and when.

Read it carefully

A visa granted is not a person settling: visas include short stays, study visas here exclude dependants, and net migration subtracts everyone who left. ONS changed how it estimates net migration in 2020.
