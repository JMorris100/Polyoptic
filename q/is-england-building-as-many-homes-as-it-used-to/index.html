Is England building as many homes as it used to?

No: England completed 142,000 homes in 2025, 40% of the 1968 peak of 353,000.

Source: MHCLG House building live tables · Open Government Licence v3.0

Open this in the explorer → See it with the other questions

The numbers

England completed 353,000 new homes in 1968, the most in any year since the war. In the last thirty years the most in a single year was 178,000, in 2022, and in 2025 it was 142,000 — 40% of the 1968 peak.

Why a columns chart

Homes completed is a count for each year, so each column is one year’s output and its height the total. Columns make the size of the post-war peak against today plain in a way a thin line does not.

The indicators

  • New homes completed142,050 dwellings per year, 2025 · England

Read it carefully

These are completions counted by councils and building control in England. They miss conversions and changes of use, which the ‘net additions’ measure includes, so they understate how many homes the stock gains.
