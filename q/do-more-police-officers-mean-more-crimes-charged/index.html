Do more police officers mean more crimes charged?

Across these 12 years, if anything the years with more officers had lower charge rates (a correlation of −0.41). Officer numbers fell to 122,000 in 2018 and rose to 148,000 by 2024; the charge rate fell from 15.5% to 5.6% in 2021-22, and is back to 8.5%. Both moved with time, for their own reasons — the dots trace a loop, not a line.

Source: Home Office Police workforce, England and Wales; Home Office Crime Outcomes in England and Wales · Open Government Licence v3.0

Why a scatter chart

Each dot is a year, placed by officer numbers across and charge rate up. If more officers meant more charges the dots would climb to the right. Joining them in time order shows what is really going on.

Read it carefully

A scatter of yearly totals cannot show cause either way. Charge rates also depend on how crimes are recorded and what kinds are reported, and both changed over these years. Officers are counted at March, charge rates by financial year.
