Why this pairing

The stock and the flow. Debt as a share of GDP is the number that gets quoted; what it costs to carry each year is the one that constrains a budget, and the two do not move together — interest tracks rates as much as it tracks borrowing.

The explorer will show you the working behind any point on this chart — the published figure, the figure being plotted, and the arithmetic between them, with a link to the release it came from. Every value is also listed on Explorer Verify.

Read it carefully

Debt interest here is interest and dividends paid to the private sector and the rest of the world, excluding public sector banks. Net debt is also excluding public sector banks.
