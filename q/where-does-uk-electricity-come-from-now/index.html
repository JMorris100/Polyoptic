Where does UK electricity come from now?

Mostly from low-carbon sources: in 2025 renewables made 52.1% of the UK’s electricity, nuclear 12.2% and fossil fuels 32.3%.

Source: DESNZ Energy Trends · Open Government Licence v3.0

The numbers

In 1998 fossil fuels made 68.5% of the UK’s electricity and renewables 2.5%. Low-carbon sources — renewables plus nuclear — overtook fossil fuels in 2017, and renewables alone did so in 2020. By 2025 renewables made 52.1% against fossil fuels’ 32.3%.

Why a line chart

Shares of the same total over a quarter of a century. A line shows exactly when one source overtook another, which a stacked chart would bury in its middle bands.

Read it carefully

Low-carbon includes renewables, so those two lines overlap by design — they are not slices that add up to 100%. The space between them is nuclear.
