Has crime gone up in England and Wales?

Not on the best measure: the Crime Survey shows crime down 65% since 2002-03, though police-recorded crime is up 49% since 2013-14.

Source: ONS Crime in England and Wales; ONS Crime in England and Wales (Home Office police recorded crime) · Open Government Licence v3.0

Open this in the explorer → See it with the other questions

The numbers

Police-recorded crime has risen 49% since its low in 2013-14 — the rise most headlines report. The Crime Survey, which asks people what happened to them and does not depend on what the police record, shows crime down 65% since 2002-03, and 78% lower than its peak in 1995-96.

Why a line chart

Two measures of the same thing at very different sizes. Rebasing both to 100 shows the direction of each, and the year they part ways.

The indicators

  • Crime Survey estimate of crime4,333 thousand incidents, 2025-26 · England and Wales
  • Police recorded crime5,235,213 offences, 2025-26 · England and Wales

Read it carefully

Police figures rose from 2014 largely because recording was tightened after inspectors found crimes going unrecorded. The survey misses crimes against businesses, only counts fraud from 2016, and paused in 2020.
