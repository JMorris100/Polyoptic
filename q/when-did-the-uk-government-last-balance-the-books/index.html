When did the UK government last balance the books?

In 2001: the government has borrowed every year since, and in 65 of the 78 years from 1948.

BorrowingSurplus

The numbers

The government borrowed in 65 of the 78 years from 1948 to 2025. The last surplus was in 2001. Borrowing peaked at 12.7% of GDP in 2020; in 2025 it was 5.0%.

Why a columns chart

Borrowing is one total per year and can go below zero when there is a surplus. Columns above and below the line show each year’s sign at a glance — and as a share of GDP, 1950 and 2020 compare fairly.

The indicators

  • Public sector net borrowing£151,407m, cash, 2025 · UK

Read it carefully

In cash terms every recent year would dwarf the past, which is why this is a share of the economy. A surplus here is the whole public sector’s, before any one-off transfers are taken out.
