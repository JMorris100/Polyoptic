About this figure

  • Life expectancy at birth, the total — chart it beside healthy life expectancy to see how many of those years are spent in good health
  • Pooled 2-year estimates, plotted at the first year of each band — a point here summarises 2 years, not one
  • Life expectancy at birth is a period measure based on mortality rates observed in the given reference period. It does not predict how long babies born during this period will actually live.
  • 2022 is the latest year the source has published here. Polyoptic checks it every Monday (last on 2026-10-19) and adds a new year when one appears.
