About this figure

  • National Rail only: Eurostar, the London Underground and light rail are not included
  • Financial years (April to March) from 1984-85. Before that ORR only has calendar years, back to 1872, and there are no figures for 1939 to 1945
  • Everything from 2020-21 onwards is provisional. ORR has not yet adjusted for split ticketing (one trip bought as several tickets, which inflates the count), and plans revisions back to April 2020. Elizabeth line journeys from 2022-23 have also been corrected once for overcounting and may be adjusted again
  • The year labelled 2020 is April 2020 to March 2021, when journeys fell to under a quarter of the pre-pandemic level

Breaks in the series

  • 1938: DfT data from 1938. Figures from 1938 come from the Department for Transport; earlier years are historical railway returns compiled by the National Infrastructure Commission
  • 1984: Calendar to financial years. Figures to 1983 are calendar years; from 1984 they are April to March financial years, so January to March 1984 is not counted in either
  • 1986: Ticket-system data from 1986. From April 1986 journeys are estimated from the CAPRI ticket-sales system, which missed some operator-specific and multi-modal tickets until 1999. From April 2003 they come from its successor, LENNON
  • 1999: PTE tickets included. From April 1999 journeys on Passenger Transport Executive tickets are included, which they were not before
  • 2020: Pandemic estimation. Mass season-ticket refunds in 2020-21 meant ORR had to estimate season and other ticket use by a different method; it marks a break for 2020-21 and 2021-22 and says these years are more uncertain than others
