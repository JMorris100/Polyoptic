About this figure

  • Age-standardised mortality from cardiovascular diseases considered preventable for people aged under 75, per 100,000 people.
  • Pooled 3-year estimates, plotted at the first year of each band — a point summarises three years, not one
  • Produced by Northern Ireland Department of Health, published through ONS Explore Local Statistics
  • All figures up to and including 2011 are based on the 2011 Census mid-year population estimates. All figures from 2012 onward are based on the 2021 Census mid-year population estimates. This indicator covers the period since March 2020 and includes the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic. Rates presented are based on year of death registration, not year of occurrence.
