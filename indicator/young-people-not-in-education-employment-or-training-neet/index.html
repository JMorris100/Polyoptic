About this figure

  • A Labour Force Survey estimate, not a count. Each point is the October to December quarter, which is the figure DfE leads its annual brief with. The data are not seasonally adjusted, so quarters should not be mixed: July to September always runs highest as school and college leavers are between courses
  • Age is academic age (age at the start of the school year), so this is not identical to the ONS UK NEET figure, which is seasonally adjusted and uses age on the survey date. DfE puts the gap at about half a point
  • Sampling error is around half a percentage point either way, so year-to-year moves smaller than that are not reliably real
  • The LFS response rate fell sharply from 2020 and ONS has reweighted the survey since; estimates from 2020 on are less certain than earlier ones
