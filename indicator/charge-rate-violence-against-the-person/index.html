About this figure

  • Measured against the offences recorded in the same period, which is the basis the headline charge rate uses. The most recent year is therefore understated — a lot of those investigations are still open, and their outcomes get assigned later
  • That lag is not even across offence types. Sexual offences take the longest to investigate, so they are depressed the most in the latest year: on the closed-investigations basis the same year reads roughly twice as high
