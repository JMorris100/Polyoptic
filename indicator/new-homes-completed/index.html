About this figure

  • Permanent dwellings completed, all tenures, from building control inspections (live table 244). Not seasonally adjusted
  • A quick indicator, not the official measure of housing supply. Net additional dwellings, which also counts conversions and changes of use and takes off demolitions, runs well above this and is the figure the 1.5 million homes target is judged on
  • The post-war peak of over 350,000 a year was mostly council housing. Councils have built only a few thousand homes a year since the 1990s
  • Data for earlier years are less reliable and definitions may not be consistent through the series. Figures from October 2005 to March 2007 miss a small number of homes inspected by independent approved inspectors. The latest year is provisional
