About this figure

  • Full-time equivalent officers in the 43 territorial forces as at 31 March each year
  • Excludes British Transport Police, special constables and officers seconded out to bodies such as the National Crime Agency. Includes officers seconded in, and those on long-term absence such as parental leave
  • Numbers fell by about 20,000 between 2010 and 2018 under austerity, then rose by a similar amount under the 2019 to 2023 uplift programme
