About this figure

  • Share of all A&E attendances — major emergency departments, single-specialty units, minor injury units and walk-in centres — that ended in admission, transfer or discharge within four hours of arrival. The operational standard is 95%, and the interim target has been 76–78%
  • Including minor injury units flatters the figure. Type 1 (major A&E) performance alone runs far lower: about 61% in 2025-26
  • Measures time spent in A&E, not how long patients waited to be seen by a clinician

Breaks in the series

  • 2015: Monthly collection. The data source switched from the weekly WSitAE return to the monthly MSitAE return from July 2015
  • 2019: CRS trial sites excluded. From May 2019 to May 2023, 14 trusts testing new A&E standards (the Clinical Review of Standards) stopped reporting four-hour performance and are excluded from the figure. NHS England warns these years are not directly comparable with others
  • 2020: COVID-19. Attendances fell by around a third in 2020-21, which flattered four-hour performance. Booked A&E appointments were also counted from August 2020
