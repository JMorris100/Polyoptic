About this figure

  • Deaths reported to the police on the STATS19 form. Fatalities are near-completely reported, so this is the most reliable series in the road safety set
  • The 1926 start predates driving tests, MOTs and speed limits on most roads; deaths peaked in 1966 at over 7,900 with a fraction of today's traffic
