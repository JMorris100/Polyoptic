About this figure

  • People neither in work nor looking for work: students, carers, the retired and the long-term sick among them
  • The headline Labour Force Survey rate, back to 1971. The Annual Population Survey version, Economic inactivity rate, 16 to 64 (APS), starts in 2004, ends sooner and has figures for local areas

Breaks in the series

  • 2024: Switch to Transformed LFS. Transition from the Labour Force Survey to the Transformed LFS changes the basis
