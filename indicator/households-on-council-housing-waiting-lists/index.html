About this figure

  • Households on local authority housing registers as at 31 March (1 April up to 2018), from live table 600
  • Not the number of households waiting for social housing. Councils purge their lists at different intervals, and a household can be on more than one council's list
  • Choice-based lettings from 2003 pushed the lists up, because applicants had a reason to register. Since 2012 councils have been able to set their own rules on who qualifies, and many shut people out

Breaks in the series

  • 2013: Localism Act qualification rules. The Localism Act 2011 let councils decide who qualifies for their register from June 2012. Many removed households with no priority, so most of the fall over the next two years is a change in who counts rather than in need
