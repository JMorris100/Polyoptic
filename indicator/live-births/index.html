About this figure

  • Births occurring in each year and registered in England and Wales, including a few hundred to mothers usually resident outside England and Wales
  • Births peaked at 881,000 in the 1947 baby boom and at 876,000 in 1964. The 2025 figure, 585,000, is the lowest since 1977
  • Birth registration was suspended for several months during the 2020 lockdown; ONS counts births by when they happened, so late-registered 2020 births are included
