About this figure

  • The average number of children a woman would have if she lived through her childbearing years at this year's birth rates. About 2.1 is needed to replace the population without migration
  • Calculated here from ONS's five-year age-specific fertility rates, because ONS no longer publishes the headline rate as a time series. It matches ONS's own figure to within about 0.01 in most years (1.39 for 2025), but can differ by up to 0.05 in a few wartime years
  • Rates from 2012 to 2020 were revised in 2023 after the 2021 Census showed more women of childbearing age than previously estimated, so older publications show slightly higher figures for those years
