About this figure

  • Each point is the position at 31 March. Full-time equivalent counts a 37.5-hour week as one, so it falls when GPs cut their hours even if headcount holds steady
  • Fully qualified GPs only: partners, salaried GPs, retainers and regular locums. GPs in training are excluded, as are ad-hoc locums, which have been removed from the whole time series since June 2021
  • Traditional general practices only — GPs working in walk-in centres, urgent treatment centres, prisons and similar settings are not counted
  • Includes NHS England's estimates for practices that did not submit full records. Early years of the collection had lower submission rates and may understate the true figure
  • Published by NHS Digital until its 2023 merger with NHS England, and by NHS England since

Breaks in the series

  • 2017: Locum recording. Recorded GP locum numbers jumped in March 2017 after new recording guidance. NHS England has since estimated the missing locum FTE back to 2015, but the early years rest partly on those estimates
  • 2025: Leaver records cleaned. From April 2025, a data-quality measure removed records of staff who had left a practice but not been closed down. NHS England says this may have reduced headline figures from that point
