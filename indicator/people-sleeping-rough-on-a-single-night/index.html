About this figure

  • A snapshot of people seen, or thought to be, sleeping rough on one night between October and November. It is not everyone who sleeps rough over a year, which is several times higher
  • Each council chooses whether to count on the street or to estimate with local partners, and picks its own night, so the method is not uniform across areas or years
  • Excludes people in hostels, night shelters and other temporary accommodation, who are homeless but not sleeping rough

Breaks in the series

  • 2020: Everyone In. In March 2020 councils were told to bring everyone sleeping rough indoors during the pandemic. The autumn 2020 and 2021 snapshots are depressed by that emergency programme rather than reflecting the usual pressures
