About this figure

  • Police-reported casualties only. DfT says plainly that non-fatal injuries are substantially under-reported — comparison with survey and hospital data suggests a large share never reaches a STATS19 form — so treat the level as a floor and the trend as the useful part
  • Slight-injury counts are also affected by forces changing their injury-reporting systems from 2016, which raised recorded severity without any change on the road
