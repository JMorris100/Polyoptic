About this figure

  • Deaths by the year they were registered, not the year they happened. Most deaths are registered within days, but those referred to a coroner can take months, so a few fall into the following year
  • Includes deaths of non-residents that occurred in England and Wales
  • A count, not a rate. The population has grown and aged, so a flat number of deaths still means mortality rates have fallen
  • The series starts with civil registration in 1838; registration was not compulsory until 1875, so the earliest years undercount

Breaks in the series

  • 1875: Registration compulsory. Death registration became compulsory under the Births and Deaths Registration Act 1874; earlier years undercount
