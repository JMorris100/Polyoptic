Every chart is editable

On the chart itself, not buried in a settings panel. These four change what a number actually means.

REBASE

Index to 100

Pick any year as the baseline and compare things measured in completely different units.

DEFLATE

Real terms

GDP deflator for spending, CPI for household figures. Cash terms is never the default.

NORMALISE

Per person, % of GDP

Population and GDP live centrally, so any series can be re-expressed on demand.

SMOOTH

Rolling average

Strip seasonality without hiding it — the raw series stays visible underneath.

Where it comes from

We publish no data of our own. Every series links back to its original release, with the licence and the date it was pulled — and every raw value is on Explorer Verify, searchable by topic and source, next to the exact endpoint it was fetched from.
