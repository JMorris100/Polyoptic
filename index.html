Every UK statistic, made comparable.

Government publishes thousands of figures across dozens of departments, in different units, on different calendars, for different parts of the country. Polyoptic brings them together and lets you rebase, adjust for inflation, compare and map them — and tells you when two numbers aren't measuring the same thing.

Where it comes from

We publish no data of our own. Every series links back to its original release, with the licence and the date it was pulled — and every raw value is on Explorer Verify, searchable by topic and source, next to the exact endpoint it was fetched from.
