UK official statistics

Every UK statistic, made comparable.

Government publishes thousands of figures across dozens of departments, in different units, on different calendars, for different parts of the country. Polyoptic brings them together and lets you rebase, adjust for inflation, compare and map them — and tells you when two numbers aren't measuring the same thing.

The warnings are the product

Putting two lines on one chart is easy. Making the comparison honest is the hard part, and it is the part nobody else bothers with. The explorer refuses to compare across a mismatch quietly.

Different geographiesEngland-only against UK-wide is not like for like, and the chart says so rather than letting the shapes imply otherwise.
Different year basesAcademic and financial years plot at the calendar year they start in, so two points on the same vertical can sit twelve months apart.
Different unitsTwo lines on separate scales can be made to look related by scaling alone. The warning offers a one-click rebase.
Inapplicable transformsYou can deflate a cash figure. You cannot deflate a rate. Transforms that would produce nonsense are greyed out, not silently applied.
Series discontinuitiesThe Labour Force Survey became the Transformed LFS; absence recording changed after 2020. The chart draws the break instead of a smooth line across it.
Devolved definitionsScottish qualifications are not GCSEs, and each nation's NHS counts waiting differently. Compare the trend inside a nation, not the level between them.

Where the numbers come from

Polyoptic publishes no original data. Everything here is collected by ONS, MHCLG, DfE, the Home Office, DESNZ, Defra, the Department for Transport, NHS England, the Bank of England, the OBR and HM Treasury, and is used under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Every chart carries its sources, its licence and a CSV download.

Figures get revised. The published bundle is committed to the repository each week, so git log data/bundle.json shows exactly when a number changed and by how much.
