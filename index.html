02

Every chart is editable

Not buried in a settings panel — on the chart itself. Most sites give you twenty chart types and no way to make a fair comparison. These four are the ones that actually change what a number means.

REBASE

Index to 100

Pick any year as the baseline and compare things measured in completely different units.

DEFLATE

Real terms

Adjusted with the GDP deflator for spending, CPI for household figures. Cash terms is never the default.

NORMALISE

Per person, % of GDP

Population and GDP live centrally, so any series can be re-expressed on demand.

SMOOTH

Rolling average

Strip seasonality without hiding it — the raw series stays visible underneath.
